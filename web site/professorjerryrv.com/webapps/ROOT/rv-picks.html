Truck and Tow Vehicle Gear

TraXion Tailgate Ladder XL

A fold-away ladder for getting into a pickup bed. The XL model has a pivoting frame designed to adjust to uneven ground.

Jerry’s take

I'm not as young as I used to be. Hopping in and out of the truck bed isn't quite as easy as it was years ago. This ladder is always at your fingertips when you need to climb in or out of the bed. It installs with a few sheet metal screws, and has a strap to hold it in place against the inside of the tailgate for travel.

Water and Plumbing

Camco EvoFlex2 25-ft RV Drinking Water Hose

A 25-foot hose designed for connecting an RV to a drinking water hookup.

Jerry’s take

I understand that nobody needs to be told they need a fresh water hose. However, if you've ever tried to roll up and pack a traditional fresh water hose in 40-degree weather after a cold front came through overnight you know how stiff those traditional hoses can become. Plus they take up more valuable space in the storage compartment. This hose is collapsable, and remains flexible even in cold weather. I've got two of these just in case there's a long run to the shore water hookup. BTW... I couldn't find an equivalent 'black tank rinse' hose in the RV section. But they are available as standard collapsable garden hoses in the Home and Garden section.

Security

2 Pcs Wheel Lock for Cars, Heavy Duty Trailer Wheel Lock Universal Security Anti Theft Tire Clamp for Vehicles Boot Camper SUV ATV Boat Motorcycle Deterrent

Jerry’s take

There is a lot of discussion in the RV community about how to protect your RV from being stolen. Some solutions cost hundreds of dollars. Where they all work to some extent, if someone wants to steal your RV bad enough, they'll do it. My philosophy falls in line with the story of the two people running from the lion saying we'll never outrun the lion. One turns to the other and says, "I don't have to outrun the lion... I just have to outrun you!". In my opinion the way to give the highest probability that your RV won't be stolen is to make it more difficult to steal than the RV sitting next to you. I use two of these claws, always both on one side of the RV. The easiest way to disable them is to remove the whole tire. But if there is a clamp on each tire on one side, your RV is not going to be rolling anywhere. Easy to install on the tires and easy to remove. I do wish they had keyed both claws in the set with the same key. But you can't having everything. Still a good product.
