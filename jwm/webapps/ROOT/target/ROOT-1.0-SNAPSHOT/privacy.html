Privacy Policy

How Professor Jerry RV handles information you choose to share.

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Professor Jerry RV respects your privacy. This policy describes the information that may be collected when you visit this website, contact us, or request RV training, service, or inspection services.

Information you provide

When you contact us by email, telephone, or a website form, you may provide your name, contact information, RV details, service location, and information about your questions or requested services. Please avoid sending payment-card details or other sensitive information through ordinary email or contact forms.

Website and technical information

Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure may record technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, dates, and diagnostic or security events. If we add analytics, advertising technology, or optional cookies, we will review and update this notice to describe those practices.

How information is used

  • To respond to questions and schedule or provide requested services.
  • To communicate about estimates, appointments, reports, and follow-up matters.
  • To maintain website performance, reliability, and security.
  • To meet applicable legal, recordkeeping, or business obligations.

Sharing information

We do not sell customer contact information as part of our ordinary business practices. Information may be shared with service providers who support hosting, communications, scheduling, payment processing, or other business operations when needed to provide services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect legitimate rights and safety.

Inspection reports and photographs

RV service and inspection work may involve photographs, notes, and reports about an RV. We use these materials to perform and document the requested work and share them with the customer or others as authorized or required. Specific report delivery and confidentiality terms may also be addressed in a service agreement.

Retention and security

Information is retained as reasonably needed for business operations, service records, dispute resolution, and applicable legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

External links

This website may link to third-party websites, including product providers and Atlas RV Solutions. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Your questions and choices

To ask about personal information you have provided, or to request a correction or deletion where applicable, contact info@ProfessorJerryRV.com. Some information may need to be retained for legal or legitimate business reasons.

Updates

We may revise this policy as the website and services evolve. The effective date above identifies the current version.
