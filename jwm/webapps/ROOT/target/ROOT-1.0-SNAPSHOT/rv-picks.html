Water and Plumbing

Camco EvoFlex2 25-ft RV Drinking Water Hose

A 25-foot hose designed for connecting an RV to a drinking water hookup.

Jerry’s take

I understand that nobody needs to be told they need a fresh water hose. However, if you've ever tried to roll up and pack a traditional fresh water hose in 40-degree weather after a cold front came through overnight you know how stiff those traditional hoses can become. Plus they take up more valuable space in the storage compartment. This hose is collapsable, and remains flexible even in cold weather. I've got two of these just in case there's a long run to the shore water hookup. BTW... I couldn't find an equivalent 'black tank rinse' hose in Amazon's RV supplies section. But they are available as standard collapsable garden hoses in the Home and Garden section.

Security

Jerry’s take

There is a lot of discussion in the RV community about how to protect your RV from being stolen. Some solutions cost hundreds of dollars. Where they all work to some extent, if someone wants to steal your RV bad enough, they'll do it. My philosophy falls in line with the story of the two people running from the lion saying we'll never outrun the lion. One turns to the other and says, "I don't have to outrun the lion... Towing Safety

Trailer Breakaway Cable,Car Accessories Heavy Duty Stainless Steel Spring Towing Coiled Wire,Travel Trailer Accessories,Suitable for Trailers,RVs,Mobile Homes,Emergency,Camper (4FT-1PCS)

Jerry’s take

That long thin RV break-away cable that triggers your RV brakes if you separate from the tow vehicle is a hugely important safety feature when towing your RV. However that cable tends to get in the way, dangle too low to the ground, tangle with the chains, etc. And it's gray color makes it easy to blend in with the pavement and accidentally get caught below your tongue jack pulling it out of the brake switch when raising the tongue. This item is an inexpensive solution. It stretches to the chain holes on your hitch. But it immediately retracts when you disconnect it.

Some notes about replacing your existing cable: The RV end of your cable 'snaps' in and out of the brake switch. I use quotes around 'snaps' because it may be in there very tight, and you might become convinced that you're damaging something by pulling hard on the cable to extract it. Trust me. That's what it's supposed to do. You will have to pull hard. Honestly, if comes out too easily I'd recommend a qualified technician take a look at it to prevent it accidentally coming out on a big bump in the road. So snap it out and snap the new one in. Warning Do not leave the clip out of the switch for a long period of time. Replace the clip immediately. While the clip is out, your emergency brakes on all RV wheels are engaged. Leaving them engaged for long periods of time could damage the brake motors or even burn them out. Plus it will drain your battery. A few minutes is fine. Hours... not so much.

Towing Gear

Aluminum RV Towing Chain Organizer

7 Way Trailer Plug & Chain Holder Keeps 7-way Plug and Chains Organized,Fit 2" & 2-1/4" Tongue Jack,Heavy Duty Anti-rust Travel Trailer Accessories,Wider Hooks

Jerry’s take

I did not buy this. One came on my new RV. It never occurred to me that an item like this was not standard equipment on all RVs. That is until a few weeks ago when a rookie camper the next site over pulled up with a fancy new top-of-the-line RV. I was explaining a few things to him on my RV and pointed out the importance of getting the chains and 7-way plug organized and off the ground immediately after disconnecting from the tow vehicle to prevent the chains getting tangled or the 7-way cable from getting caught, cut, and shorted out under the tongue jack foot (Don't ask me to discuss how I know about this so well....). We walked over to his new rig, and it wasn't there.

I strongly recommend getting one of these chain / 7-way cable caddies. I'm referencing one that bolts onto the tongue frame like mine does. But that will require some serious steel drilling. Amazon actually has more options for caddies that mount to the tongue jack column. But either way, if your bumper pull RV didn't come with one, it's an inexpensive way to protect your 7-way cable.
