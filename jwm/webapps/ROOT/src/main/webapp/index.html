Why Professor Jerry?

An engineer who enjoys teaching

RV systems can feel mysterious. They become much less intimidating when someone explains how the pieces work together.

Professor Jerry RV combines engineering discipline, real-world RV experience, and a teaching-first approach. The goal is not merely to solve today’s problem—it is to help you understand your RV well enough to recognize tomorrow’s.

  • Plain-English explanations
  • Methodical troubleshooting
  • No-pressure recommendations
  • Practical owner education
