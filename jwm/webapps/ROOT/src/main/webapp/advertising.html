Advertising & Affiliate Disclosure

Transparency about recommendations, featured products, and paid placements.

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Professor Jerry RV is built around practical education and clear explanations. When we discuss products, services, or business partners, we want visitors to understand whether a commercial relationship is involved.

Sponsored content and paid placements

Some website areas may feature paid advertisements, sponsored listings, or partner promotions. When a placement is paid or sponsored, we intend to identify it clearly using language such as Sponsored or Advertisement.

Affiliate links and referral compensation

Some links may be affiliate or referral links. If a visitor purchases through such a link, Professor Jerry RV may receive compensation at no additional cost to the visitor. We will identify material affiliate or referral relationships where they are relevant to a recommendation.

Product recommendations

Our goal is to recommend products and services based on usefulness, suitability, and real-world RV considerations—not simply compensation. A product mention is not a guarantee of performance, availability, compatibility, or suitability for every RV.

Atlas RV Solutions

Atlas RV Solutions is a related Professor Jerry project developing RV storage and organization products. References to Atlas products are not independent third-party endorsements; this relationship is disclosed so visitors can evaluate those recommendations in context.

Independent inspections

Advertising and referral relationships should not determine the findings of an RV inspection. Inspection observations and reports are intended to reflect the condition and limitations of the RV examined, rather than the interests of advertisers or sellers. Any actual or potential conflict of interest should be disclosed before accepting an engagement.

Third-party claims

Third-party advertisers and merchants are responsible for their own product descriptions, warranties, pricing, and claims. Visitors should verify important details directly with the provider.
