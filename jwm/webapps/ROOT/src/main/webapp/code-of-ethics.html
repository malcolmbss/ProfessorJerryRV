Code of Ethics

The principles guiding RV education, service, and independent inspections.

Professor Jerry RV is committed to providing clear information, responsible service, and fair, objective observations. The following principles describe how we intend to conduct our business.

Honesty and transparency

We communicate findings and recommendations truthfully, distinguish observed facts from opinions, and explain material limitations or uncertainties. We do not knowingly misrepresent an RV’s condition or the scope of work performed.

Independence and objectivity

RV inspection findings should be based on the inspection performed, not on pressure from buyers, sellers, dealers, or advertisers. We disclose material conflicts of interest and decline or appropriately manage engagements where independence cannot be maintained.

Competence and scope

We accept work consistent with our training, qualifications, tools, and applicable requirements. When a problem calls for specialized expertise or licensed work outside our scope, we explain that limitation and recommend an appropriate qualified professional.

Safety first

We place the safety of owners, occupants, and technicians ahead of convenience. We do not encourage unsafe shortcuts, and we identify safety concerns when they are discovered within the agreed scope of work.

Education and respect

We explain RV systems in understandable terms, encourage questions, and treat customers with patience and respect. Our purpose is to help owners make informed decisions, not to overwhelm them with jargon or unnecessary services.

Clear expectations and fair dealing

We strive to describe the scope, limitations, fees, and deliverables of an engagement before work begins. We communicate significant changes and avoid recommending work solely to generate additional revenue.

Confidentiality

We handle customer information, photographs, and reports with care and share them only as authorized or otherwise permitted or required. Specific confidentiality obligations may also be established in the service agreement.

Professional standards

Where applicable, inspections and technical services will be conducted in accordance with the standards, agreements, and legal requirements governing the particular engagement. This page expresses our business commitments; it does not replace any applicable professional code, inspection standard, or signed agreement.

Questions or concerns

If you have a concern about our conduct or a service we provided, please contact info@ProfessorJerryRV.com so we can review it.
