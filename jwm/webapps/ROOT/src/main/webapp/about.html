The Story Behind the Name

Meet Professor Jerry

Some people are born builders. Others are born teachers. Jerry has spent his life doing both.

After earning bachelor's and master's degrees in Electrical Engineering at the University of Texas at Austin, Jerry joined IBM, where he spent 31 years, retiring as a Senior Software Architect. During that time, he traveled throughout the United States and around the world, helping customers understand complex technology through technical seminars, product demonstrations, and hands-on training.

When he retired from IBM, teaching remained a natural calling. Jerry joined the McCombs School of Business at The University of Texas at Austin as a professor of Information Technology. It was there that students first began calling him "Professor Jerry"—a name that has followed him ever since.

In 2025, Jerry and his wife, Penney, purchased their first travel trailer and discovered what millions of RV owners already know: owning an RV is both incredibly rewarding and surprisingly complex. During their first year and a half, they traveled more than 13,000 miles while learning firsthand what it means to own, maintain, troubleshoot, and enjoy an RV.

Instead of simply accepting the learning curve, Jerry did what engineers do—he immersed himself in it.

He attended the National RV Training Academy, earning certification as a Registered RV Technician. Wanting to go even further, he completed the National RV Inspectors Academy and became a Certified RV Inspector in June 2026.

Coming from a family of educators, Jerry has always believed that the best way to help someone is to teach them why something works—not just how to fix it.

Today, Professor Jerry combines decades of engineering experience, a lifetime of teaching, and nationally recognized RV certifications to help RV owners become more confident, more knowledgeable, and ultimately enjoy their RVs with fewer surprises.

Whether you're buying your first RV, learning how its systems work, or trying to solve a frustrating problem, Professor Jerry's goal is simple:

Help you understand your RV with confidence.
